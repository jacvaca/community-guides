# Community Engagement Metrics: Quick Reference Guide

## Core Health Metrics

### Activity Metrics

**Monthly Active Users (MAU)**
- **Calculation**: (Number of users active in last 30 days / Total members) × 100
- **Why it matters**: Shows overall community vitality and engagement breadth
- **Impact**: Indicates if community is growing, stable, or declining

**Stickiness Score**
- **Calculation**: Daily Active Users / Monthly Active Users
- **Why it matters**: Measures engagement quality and frequency
- **Impact**: Higher ratio means members return more often, showing stronger habit formation

**Post Frequency**
- **Calculation**: Total posts or comments / Number of active members
- **Why it matters**: Shows depth of member contribution
- **Impact**: Indicates if community culture encourages participation

**Average Session Length**
- **Calculation**: Total time spent / Number of sessions
- **Why it matters**: Reveals if members find content compelling
- **Impact**: Longer sessions = deeper engagement with community value

---

## Value Metrics

**Net Promoter Score (NPS)**
- **Calculation**: % Promoters (score 9-10) - % Detractors (score 0-6)
- **Why it matters**: Measures member satisfaction and likelihood to recommend
- **Impact**: Strong predictor of growth through word-of-mouth

**Value Realization Rate**
- **Calculation**: Survey members on 1-10 scale: "Are you getting the value you expected?"
- **Why it matters**: Shows if community delivers on its promise
- **Impact**: Directly correlates with retention and engagement

**Time to First Value**
- **Calculation**: Days from joining to first meaningful interaction/benefit
- **Why it matters**: Fast value = higher activation and retention
- **Impact**: Reduces early churn, improves onboarding effectiveness

---

## Retention & Growth Metrics

**Member Retention Rate**
- **Calculation**: (Members at end of period / Members at start) × 100
- **Why it matters**: Shows community's ability to keep members engaged long-term
- **Impact**: Retention is cheaper than acquisition; high retention = sustainable growth

**Churn Rate**
- **Calculation**: (Members who left or went inactive / Total members at start) × 100
- **Why it matters**: Identifies if community is losing members faster than gaining them
- **Impact**: High churn signals value gaps or poor experience

**Referral Activity**
- **Calculation**: Number of new members invited by existing members
- **Why it matters**: Indicates strong brand advocacy and organic growth
- **Impact**: Reduces acquisition costs, attracts high-quality members

---

## Belonging Metrics

**Psychological Safety Score**
- **Calculation**: Survey: "Do you feel safe sharing ideas/questions?" (1-10 scale)
- **Why it matters**: Safe environments drive participation and vulnerability
- **Impact**: Increases quality of interactions and depth of relationships

**Voice Heard Score**
- **Calculation**: Survey: "Do you feel your input matters?" (1-10 scale)
- **Why it matters**: Members who feel heard stay engaged and contribute more
- **Impact**: Drives participation rates and member investment

**Relationship Formation Rate**
- **Calculation**: Survey: "Have you formed meaningful connections?" (Yes/No) × 100
- **Why it matters**: Relationships are strongest retention driver
- **Impact**: Members with friends in community are significantly less likely to churn

---

## Business Impact Metrics

**Support Deflection Rate**
- **Calculation**: (Questions answered by community / Total questions) × 100
- **Why it matters**: Reduces support costs through peer-to-peer help
- **Impact**: Direct cost savings on support team resources
- **Benchmark**: Salesforce achieves 83% peer resolution

**Community-Sourced Leads**
- **Calculation**: Number of qualified leads from community activities
- **Why it matters**: Measures acquisition impact
- **Impact**: Lowers customer acquisition cost (CAC)

**Content Contribution Volume**
- **Calculation**: Number of user-generated posts, guides, templates, etc.
- **Why it matters**: Reduces content production costs
- **Impact**: Scales value creation without proportional resource increase

**Event Participation Rate**
- **Calculation**: (Event attendees / Total invites) × 100
- **Why it matters**: Shows engagement depth and program effectiveness
- **Impact**: Events drive relationships, learning, and brand affinity

---

## Engagement Depth Metrics

**Comment Quality Score**
- **Calculation**: Average comment length + sentiment analysis
- **Why it matters**: Distinguishes meaningful engagement from vanity metrics
- **Impact**: High-quality interactions drive real value exchange

**Contributor Distribution**
- **Calculation**: 
  - Power Users (Top 10%): Percentage creating most content
  - Regular (11-30%): Moderate contributors
  - Occasional (31-60%): Light participants  
  - Lurkers (60%+): Passive consumers
- **Why it matters**: Shows community health and participation balance
- **Impact**: Healthy communities have growing contributor tiers

**Repeat Participation Rate**
- **Calculation**: (Members active in consecutive periods / Total active members) × 100
- **Why it matters**: Measures habit formation and sustained engagement
- **Impact**: Repeat participants are more valuable and loyal

---

## Sentiment Metrics

**Overall Sentiment Score**
- **Calculation**: Automated analysis of comment/post tone (positive/neutral/negative)
- **Why it matters**: Early warning system for community health issues
- **Impact**: Allows proactive intervention before problems escalate

**Detractor Response Time**
- **Calculation**: Average time to address negative feedback or complaints
- **Why it matters**: Shows responsiveness to member concerns
- **Impact**: Fast response prevents negative sentiment from spreading

---

## Conversion & Advocacy Metrics

**Member-to-Customer Conversion**
- **Calculation**: (Community members who became customers / Total members) × 100
- **Why it matters**: Direct revenue impact from community
- **Impact**: Demonstrates ROI of community investment

**Customer Lifetime Value (LTV) Multiplier**
- **Calculation**: Average LTV of community members vs. non-members
- **Why it matters**: Shows premium value of engaged community members
- **Impact**: Justifies community investment through increased revenue per customer

**Advocacy Actions**
- **Calculation**: Count of reviews written, social shares, case studies, referrals
- **Why it matters**: Measures organic marketing impact
- **Impact**: Free marketing and credibility from authentic member voices

---

## Operational Efficiency Metrics

**Response Time**
- **Calculation**: Average time from question posted to first helpful response
- **Why it matters**: Faster responses = better member experience
- **Impact**: Improves satisfaction and encourages continued participation

**Resolution Rate**
- **Calculation**: (Questions answered satisfactorily / Total questions) × 100
- **Why it matters**: Shows community's ability to solve member problems
- **Impact**: Reduces need for formal support resources

**Moderation Efficiency**
- **Calculation**: Moderator actions per 100 members
- **Why it matters**: Indicates community self-governance vs. need for oversight
- **Impact**: Lower moderation needs = healthier culture and lower operational costs

---

## Cohort Analysis Metrics

**New Member Activation Rate**
- **Calculation**: (New members active in first 30 days / Total new members) × 100
- **Why it matters**: Predicts long-term retention from onboarding quality
- **Impact**: Activated members are significantly more likely to stay engaged

**Cohort Retention by Join Date**
- **Calculation**: Track retention of members who joined in same month over time
- **Why it matters**: Reveals if community experience improves or degrades over time
- **Impact**: Identifies which cohorts had best/worst experience for optimization

**Engagement by Member Tenure**
- **Calculation**: Average activity level grouped by how long members have been in community
- **Why it matters**: Shows if engagement increases or decreases over time
- **Impact**: Helps design programs for different tenure stages

---

## Key Benchmarks

**Healthy Community Indicators:**
- MAU: 14-18% for mature communities, 50%+ for new/curated communities
- NPS: 50+ is excellent, 30-50 is good, <30 needs improvement
- Monthly Retention: 85%+ is strong
- Churn: <10% monthly is healthy
- Response Time: <24 hours for active communities
- Contributor Ratio: 5-10% of members actively creating content

**Note**: Benchmarks vary significantly by community type, size, and industry. Track your own trends over time rather than fixating on absolute numbers.

## Quick Metric Prioritization

**Start With These 5:**
1. Monthly Active Users (MAU)
2. Net Promoter Score (NPS)
3. Retention Rate
4. Value Realization Rate
5. Response Time

**Add When Scaling:**
- Engagement depth metrics
- Business impact metrics specific to your goals
- Cohort analysis
- Sentiment tracking

**Advanced/Mature Communities:**
- Full contributor distribution analysis
- Detailed cohort retention tracking
- Advocacy action measurement
- Cross-platform engagement correlation

---

## How to Use These Metrics

1. **Pick 3-5 primary metrics** aligned with your community goals
2. **Track consistently** (monthly minimum, quarterly surveys)
3. **Set realistic targets** based on your baseline and community maturity
4. **Look for trends** more than absolute numbers
5. **Combine quantitative data** with qualitative feedback from member conversations
6. **Adjust your approach** based on what the data reveals

**Remember**: Metrics should inform decisions, not replace human judgment. The goal is a healthy, valuable community—not perfect numbers.
